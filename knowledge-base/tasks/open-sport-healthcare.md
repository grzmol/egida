## 2. Challenge
**[SPEC]**
Build a tool, application, system, or prototype that helps people take an informed and active role in their health, physical activity, or wellbeing. Pick a specific user group and a concrete need; make a meaningful activity or decision easier.
- Example directions (non-limiting): understanding patterns in activity, recovery, daily routines; sustainable exercise habits adapted to abilities/circumstances; easier preparation for healthcare appointments and communication with professionals; organizing health information and sharing details with caregivers; improving access to physical activity and wellbeing support.
- Target users may be individuals, families, sports communities, healthcare professionals, or organizations.

**[NOTE]**
Problem framing: training records, habits, wellbeing observations, and healthcare information are hard to connect; information often lacks clear guidance.

## 3. Requirements & Constraints
**[SPEC]**
- Focus on a clear user journey; demonstrate where practical value is delivered.
- Consider accessibility, motivation, and effort required for regular use.
- Show how users understand their situation and take an achievable next step.
- No task-specific tech stack, data, or legal constraints.
