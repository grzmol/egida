## 2. Challenge
**[SPEC]**
Build a solution where AI plays a meaningful role and addresses a specific user need. Existing models, APIs, tools allowed, combined with own components or own approach. Open to any industry/technology, practical or unconventional.
**[NOTE]**
Example directions: making complex information easier to find/understand; supporting learning and adapting knowledge presentation; improving accessibility of content/services; assisting creative work and idea development; automating workflow stages; helping users analyze situations and compare actions.

## 3. Requirements & Constraints
**[SPEC]**
- Show: the role AI plays, how components work together, benefits provided.
- Explain: technical decisions, capabilities and limitations, how users can verify outputs and remain in control.
- Demonstrate via a concrete use case that makes the value easy to understand.
