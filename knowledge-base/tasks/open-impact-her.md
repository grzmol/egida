## 2. Challenge
**[SPEC]**
- Build a technology-driven solution addressing a real challenge affecting women, with potential for positive change.
- Open field, technology and approach; team chooses the problem.
- Example areas: safety, health, education, professional development, access to services, participation in the digital world.
- Start from a well-identified need; show how the solution responds to it (remove barriers, improve accessibility, expand opportunities, ease everyday life).
- Show value, real-life use, and intended positive impact.
