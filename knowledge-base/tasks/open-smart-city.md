## 2. Challenge
**[SPEC]**
- Build a tool, application, system, or prototype addressing a specific problem of a city or its residents, helping people make better use of urban resources and services.
- Choose the situation to improve and identify the users.
- Example areas: transportation & journey planning; accessibility of spaces/services for people with different needs; management of energy, water, waste, shared infrastructure; resident–institution communication; urban data for decisions; responding to failures, disruptions, emergencies.
- Scale: single street, neighbourhood, service, or whole city — matching the problem.
**[NOTE]**
Framing: a city is a system of interconnected services, infrastructure and everyday decisions; small improvements can help many people.

## 3. Requirements & Constraints
**[SPEC]**
- Show how the idea works in a concrete situation and who benefits.
- Consider accuracy and timeliness of information, ease of use, and possibility of testing in real urban conditions.
- No mandated tech stack or data.
