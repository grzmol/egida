## 2. Challenge
**[SPEC]**
- Build a tool, application, system or prototype that strengthens security and resilience: prevent threats, detect them earlier, or reduce their consequences.
- Choose a specific problem and identify who faces it; focus on a realistic scenario.
- Example directions (non-binding; other approaches welcome if they address a defence/security need):
  - Help smaller organizations identify weaknesses and prioritize protective measures.
  - Support verification of suspicious messages, content or information sources.
  - Improve coordination and information sharing during emergencies.
  - Map dependencies between services and identify potential points of failure.
  - Make security procedures easier to understand and follow.

**[NOTE]**
Consider incomplete information, limited resources, and unavailable services. A strong project demonstrates how it improves preparedness, response or continuity.
