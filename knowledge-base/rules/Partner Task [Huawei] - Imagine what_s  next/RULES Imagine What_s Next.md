# Huawei Challenge: Imagine What’s Next

**HackYeah 2026 Task Partner Challenge Rules**

## 1. About the Challenge

Huawei Polska sp. z o.o., ul. Domaniewska 39A, 02-672 Warsaw, Poland (“Huawei”), organizes the Huawei Challenge: Imagine What’s Next (the “Challenge”) as a Partner Competition within HackYeah 2026, taking place on 3–4 October 2026 at Tauron Arena Kraków.

Huawei is the organizer of the Challenge and funds the prizes awarded in the Challenge. PROIDEA sp. z o.o. ("PROIDEA") is the organizer of HackYeah 2026 and provides the organizational framework and the submission platform for the Challenge. PROIDEA pays the prizes to the winners on behalf of Huawei, using funds provided by Huawei under the partnership arrangements. Huawei remains responsible for these Challenge Rules, the evaluation of submissions and the selection of winners. PROIDEA does not participate in the substantive evaluation of submissions or the selection of winners.

These Challenge Rules supplement the general HackYeah 2026 Rules. All matters not specifically covered by these Challenge Rules are governed by the HackYeah 2026 Rules.

Participation in the Challenge is open only to registered HackYeah 2026 participants and implies acceptance of these Challenge Rules.

## 2. Challenge objective

The objective of the Challenge is to create an innovative mobile feature or application for the HarmonyOS ecosystem that demonstrates new ideas and possibilities enabled by a new generation of operating systems.

The detailed Challenge description, including technical requirements and suggested areas of exploration, will be published on the HackYeah website (www.hackyeah.pl) on 3 October 2026, at the time indicated in the official HackYeah 2026 schedule.

Participants are encouraged to experiment, create original concepts and demonstrate a working prototype or other convincing implementation of their idea.

## 3. Teams

Participants may compete individually or in teams of up to 6 people. References to a "team" in these Challenge Rules apply accordingly to a Participant competing individually.

Only persons registered as HackYeah 2026 participants may be members of a team.

Persons directly involved in organizing or evaluating the Huawei Challenge may not participate in the Challenge.

## 4. Challenge duration and submissions

The Challenge follows the official HackYeah 2026 schedule published by PROIDEA.

Solutions must be submitted before the official submission deadline through the platform specified by HackYeah.

The detailed submission requirements will be provided together with the Challenge description. They may include a project description, presentation, screenshots, demo, prototype, source-code repository or other materials necessary for the Jury to evaluate the solution. All submissions, presentations, demonstrations and project documentation submitted for evaluation must be prepared in English.

Submissions received after the deadline will not be considered.

The submitted solution should be created or substantially developed during the Challenge. Participants may use existing tools, frameworks, libraries, open-source software and other third-party components, provided that their use complies with the applicable licences and third-party rights.

Participants may use generative AI tools, AI coding assistants, pre-existing code, templates, boilerplate and other materials prepared before the Challenge, provided that they are legally entitled to use such materials. The requirement that the solution be created or substantially developed during the Challenge refers to the solution as a whole and does not exclude the use of such materials. Participants shall identify material pre-existing or third-party components, as well as the use of AI tools, in their submission documentation.

## 5. Jury and evaluation

Solutions will be evaluated by a Jury appointed for the Huawei Challenge.

Each Jury member assesses each submission on a scale of 1 to 10 under each of the criteria listed below. The final score of a submission is the weighted average of these assessments, calculated using the weights indicated below and averaged across all Jury members:

- originality – 20%;
- demonstrated usefulness of the proposed solution – 20%;
- technical execution – 20%;
- use or enhancement of platform capabilities – 20%;
- quality of the demonstration – 10%;
- reproducibility and transparency of the development workflow – 10%.

For the Huawei Challenge, the evaluation criteria specified in this Section 5 replace the general evaluation criteria specified in point 5.5 of the HackYeah 2026 Rules.

The Jury may invite selected teams to present or demonstrate their solutions.

The Jury’s decisions are final.

The Jury may decide not to award one or more prizes if the submitted solutions do not reach an adequate level of quality, in particular where the solution that would qualify for the respective prize does not achieve at least 50% of the maximum possible final score.

## 6. Prizes

The total prize pool for the Huawei Challenge is PLN 25,000:

- 1st place – PLN 12,000
- 2nd place – PLN 8,000
- 3rd place – PLN 5,000

The results of the Challenge will be announced on 4 October 2026 during the HackYeah 2026 closing ceremony. Each monetary prize is awarded jointly to the members of the awarded team, as indicated in the team's submission, and is divided equally among them. The prizes are paid by PROIDEA sp. z o.o. to the bank accounts indicated by the individual team members within 60 days from the announcement of the results, provided that the team members have supplied the data necessary to make the payment and to fulfil the related tax obligations. If such data is supplied later, the 60-day period runs from the date on which it is supplied.

The amounts of the prizes are reduced by any taxes, contributions and fees due in accordance with applicable law.

- provides false or misleading information;
