# OPEN TASK
# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

## Description

Artificial intelligence creates new opportunities to work with information, produce content, learn, and perform complex tasks. Its value depends on how it is applied and the benefits it brings to the people using a solution.

Your task is to create a solution in which AI plays a meaningful role and helps address a specific user need. You may use existing models, APIs, and tools, combine them with your own components, or develop your own approach.

Possible directions include:

- Making complex information easier to find and understand.
- Supporting learning and adapting how knowledge is presented.
- Improving the accessibility of content and services.
- Assisting creative work and the development of ideas.
- Automating selected stages of a workflow.
- Helping users analyze situations and compare possible actions.

The category is open to different industries, technologies, and practical or unconventional applications of AI.

Show the role AI plays in your project, how its components work together, and what benefits they provide. Explain your technical decisions, the capabilities and limitations of the solution, and how users can verify its outputs and remain in control. Demonstrate these aspects through a concrete use case that makes the project’s value easy to understand.

## Judging criteria

Our Judges will evaluate each task in five categories:

### Idea & Innovation - 30%

This criterion evaluates the uniqueness and creativity of the project's concept. Judges will look for groundbreaking ideas and inventive solutions that stand out and demonstrate a high level of innovation.

### Relation to Category - 20%

Judges assess how well the project aligns with the specific category or theme of the given task within the hackathon. It is crucial that the project clearly fits within the defined scope, demonstrating relevance to and fulfillment of the objectives associated with this particular category.

### Practical Applicability / Usability - 20%

This criterion evaluates the project's real-world utility and ease of use. Judges will consider how practical and functional the solution is, with an emphasis on its ability to address real-world needs effectively and provide a seamless user experience.

### Design - 20%

The design criterion evaluates the visual appeal and aesthetics of the project. Judges will assess the overall look and feel of the project, including the design of the user interface, graphics, and any other visual elements.

### Completeness & Implementation Value - 10%

This criterion examines the overall completeness of the project and its readiness for implementation. Judges will consider not only the technical robustness but also whether the project is fully developed, and if it has potential for successful implementation in real-world scenarios.

## The use of AI

Artificial intelligence is now a natural part of the modern software development process; therefore, its use during HackYeah is permitted. Participants may use AI-based tools, including when developing concepts, conducting research, programming, debugging, designing, preparing documentation, and at other stages of working on their project.

The team remains fully responsible for the entire submitted solution. The use of AI does not limit the team’s responsibility for the originality, functionality, security, licensing compliance, or legal compliance of the solution.

Any significant use of AI tools, as well as external models, APIs, datasets, libraries, and other resources, should be disclosed as part of the project submission. Teams are not required to provide their prompt history or specify what proportion of the project was generated using AI. Projects will not be evaluated based on the number of lines of code written independently by participants. The jury will assess the actual technical work carried out during HackYeah, in particular the complexity of the solution, its architecture, integrations, implementation approach, functionality, and the team’s level of understanding of the system they created.

The team must be able to explain and defend the technical decisions made and demonstrate that it understands the submitted solution, including any elements generated or created with the support of AI. Using AI to create functionalities that the team is unable to reasonably explain may negatively affect the technical evaluation of the project.

Participants must clearly distinguish work completed during HackYeah from elements of the project that existed before the event began and may not present previously prepared solutions as work completed during the hackathon. The use of open-source libraries, frameworks, APIs, models, and other general-purpose tools is permitted, in accordance with their applicable licences and terms of use.

Plagiarism, unauthorised use of third-party intellectual property, knowingly misleading the jury or the organiser regarding the scope of the work completed, or failure to disclose a significant part of a pre-existing solution may result in disqualification.

Partners responsible for individual challenges may establish additional rules regarding the use of AI tools, models, datasets, or other external resources within their challenge. If such rules are introduced, they take precedence over this general policy with respect to the relevant challenge.
